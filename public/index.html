🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our highest priority. SYNEXA is built on trust and modern P2P technology.

  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store, host, or see your files. Your data never touches our servers.
  • Direct Transfer: Files are transferred directly between your browser and the receiver's browser using WebRTC.
  • Connection Only: Our server is only used to connect the two devices initially. Once connected, the server steps out of the way.

🛠️ Tech Support Center

Q: What is the maximum file size?

A: Since SYNEXA is a P2P platform, there is technically no file size limit! However, very large files (e.g., 5GB+) might depend on your browser's available RAM and internet stability.

Q: Why is my transfer stuck at 99%?

A: When a transfer reaches 99%, your browser is assembling all the chunks back into a single file. For large files, this takes a few extra seconds. Just wait a moment, and the download button will appear.

Q: Does SYNEXA work on mobile browsers?

A: Yes! SYNEXA works smoothly on modern mobile browsers. Just make sure you keep the browser tab open and your phone screen awake during the transfer.
